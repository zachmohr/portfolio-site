Roto Toilet Paper Holder

Roto is my first consumer product and the first product launch from Figura Studio, my home goods design company. The design focuses on one-handed operation to make toilet paper roll changes faster, cleaner, and more accessible in everyday use. I filed for patent protection in April 2025, and this is an active ongoing program with future material iterations and collaborative versions planned with other creators and brands.
