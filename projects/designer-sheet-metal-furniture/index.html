Designer Sheet Metal Furniture Study

A playful September 2025 design exercise: reinterpreting iconic furniture in low-cost sheet metal to make high-design forms feel more approachable. I translated the Charles and Ray Eames lounge chair and the Noguchi coffee table into bend-ready metal concepts to push my sheet metal modeling skills while preserving the spirit of the originals.
