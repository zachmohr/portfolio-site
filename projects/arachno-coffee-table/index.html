Arachno Coffee Table

Second-generation application of the brazed steel rod joinery technique developed in the Claro Walnut Coffee Table — this time constrained into a rectangular, grid-like base geometry. Where the original base was organic and radial, the Arachno base imposes order on the chaos: rods cut, bent, and brazed mid-air into a repeating structural lattice. Topped with a walnut slab and epoxy river pour. The feet are custom-turned on the lathe from offcuts of the same top slab, tying the wood and steel together as one cohesive piece.
