Brass Wire Joinery Tables

100% original design — my favorite pieces. Invented a technique of bending brass rods into 90-degree corners and packing them into the steel tube construction to create a decorative structural joint. Built the side table first, then loved the technique so much it expanded into a matching console table. Walnut round top on a steel and brass frame.
