Side Table Home Garden

A fully automatic indoor garden disguised as a side table, designed for my Innovation Studio 1 graduate course. The prompt was to make fresh produce more accessible to people in food deserts — rather than adding another appliance to the home, this integrates a garden into existing furniture. The prototype features a soil moisture sensor, grow lights, a peristaltic pump for automated watering, and a camera for machine vision plant monitoring, all connected to the web for remote access. The lid is height-adjustable for easy access to the garden. This was a rapid prototyping class focused on the thinking process over a polished final product.
