Lightship Mikros Concept Camper

Conceptual compact camper study completed in March 2026, inspired by the current Lightship RV lineup and its simple utilitarian design language. I wanted to explore that same design DNA in a much smaller form factor, with a focus on highly detailed CAD development and interior packaging decisions. Key features include a fold-out couch/bed system and a telescoping canopy that raises the small camper to full stand-up height. This log keeps the image set intentionally tight so the interactive 3D model can be the centerpiece.
