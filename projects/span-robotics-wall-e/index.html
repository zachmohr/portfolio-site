SPAN Robotics

SPAN Robotics started as a personal challenge in January 2026: build a real-life WALL-E that picks up litter on its own. I designed and built the prototype from scratch, with a 3D-printed chassis, a servo-driven arm, a Jetson Orin Nano vision stack trained to find trash, and PS5 teleoperation for testing. Partnering with a fellow MID classmate, I turned the robot into a company: an autonomous waste collection system that pairs solar-powered trash stations with rovers and drones to keep outdoor spaces clean and generate environmental data. We pitched SPAN at Wichita State's 2026 Shocker New Venture Competition and won second place.
